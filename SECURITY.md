# Security Policy

This policy covers every Guard Core project that has no SECURITY.md of its own, including the hosted app at [app.guard-core.com](https://app.guard-core.com) and its tracker [saas-issues](https://github.com/Guard-Core/saas-issues). Each library repository has its own SECURITY.md with its supported versions.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue, discussion or pull request for a vulnerability.

- A library: open a private report in that repository (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability").
- The hosted app, the playground or anything else: email security@guard-core.com.

Include what is affected (project and version, or the URL), the impact, steps to reproduce and any workaround you know of.

I will acknowledge your report within 48 hours and send a detailed response within 7 days, including the next steps. I credit reporters in the advisory once the fix is out, unless you prefer to stay anonymous.

The machine-readable contact is [guard-core.com/.well-known/security.txt](https://guard-core.com/.well-known/security.txt).
